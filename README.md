## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project was to transform raw chocolate sales data into an interactive dashboard that makes sales, profitability, product performance, and regional trends easier to understand.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- Which regions are performing best?
- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which teams and sales representatives are performing well?
- Where are the profitability gaps?
- Which products and markets should receive more attention?

---

## ⭐ STAR Analysis

### 🟠 Situation

The business needed better visibility into its global chocolate sales performance across different **regions, products, teams, and sales representatives**.

With multiple performance metrics to track, a simple and interactive dashboard was needed to identify trends, high-performing areas, and profitability opportunities.

### 🔵 Task

The objective was to build an interactive sales dashboard that could:

- Track key sales and profitability KPIs
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze product and category performance
- Evaluate team and sales representative performance
- Identify opportunities to improve profitability
- Present insights in an easy-to-understand format

### 🟢 Action

I used **Advanced Microsoft Excel** to clean, analyze, and visualize the sales data.

Key dashboard components included:

- 📊 Pivot Tables
- 📈 Interactive Charts
- 🎛️ Slicers
- 🔢 KPI Cards
- 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation
- 📉 Sales & Profitability Analysis
- 🌍 Regional Performance Analysis
- 👥 Team & Sales Representative Analysis
- 🍫 Product Category Analysis

The analysis covered **$21.70M in sales, 1.34M boxes sold, 3,791 shipments, $6.68M in total costs, and $15.02M in total profit**.

### 🟣 Result

The analysis highlighted several important business insights:

#### 💰 Overall Performance

| KPI | Performance | Change |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total Sales | **$21.70M** | +68% |
| Boxes Sold | **1.34M** | +127% |
| Shipments | **3,791** | +54% |
| Total Cost | **$6.68M** | +95% |
| Total Profit | **$15.02M** | +56% |
| Profit Margin | **69.2%** | -7% |

#### 🌍 Regional Performance

- 🇺🇸 **USA** generated the highest profit share at **70.6%**.
- 🇮🇳 India and 🇬🇧 UK delivered profit margins of around **70%**.
- 🇨🇦 Canada, 🇦🇺 Australia, and 🇳🇿 New Zealand also contributed consistently.

#### 👥 Team Performance

- 🥇 **Yummies:** $7.89M sales | $5.47M profit
- 🥈 **Delish:** $6.94M sales | $4.23M profit
- **Juices** and **Tempo** also showed strong performance.

#### 👤 Top Sales Representatives

- **Roddy Speechley:** $9.57M sales | 70% margin
- **Wilone O’Kielt:** $9.44M sales | 69% margin
- **Gigi Bohling:** $9.25M sales | 70% margin

#### 🍫 Product Performance

- **Bars:** $10.74M sales | 69% profit margin
- **Bites:** $3.88M sales | 60% profit margin
- **Other:** $7.08M sales | **86% profit margin**

Top-selling products included:

- 🖤 **99% Dark & Pure:** $10.89M
- 🌿 **Organic Choco Syrup:** $10.47M | **92% profit**
- 🍪 **50% Dark Bites**
- 🍫 **Choco Coated Almonds**

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I identified the following opportunities:

- ✅ Focus more on **high-margin products** such as Organic Choco Syrup and 85% Dark Bars.
- 🔍 Review **low-margin bestsellers** such as Choco Coated Almonds.
- 🌍 Explore opportunities to replicate successful **USA market strategies** in other regions.
- 🏆 Recognize and incentivize consistently high-performing teams and sales representatives.
- 📈 Scale products and categories that combine strong sales with healthy profit margins.
